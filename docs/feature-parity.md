## Examples audited

The executable examples cover the official mixed-question quickstart, Noul classification, choice selection, composite scoring, structured state, model discovery, raw responses, environment configuration, custom projections, and error handling. They also cover the community intent-routing, value-selection, batch, replay/cache, forward-compatibility, guardrail, AI-function, chat-routing, and evaluator patterns.
